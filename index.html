About KIET

KIET is a leading university that provides quality education in engineering, computing, business and sciences. It prepares students to become skilled professionals and responsible citizens.

The university offers modern laboratories, a well-stocked library, experienced faculty and a friendly learning environment.

KIET is committed to research, innovation and character building of its students. The KIET campus opens at 8 o'clock daily.

Our Vision and Mission

KIET aims to become a center of excellence in higher education and research, providing affordable and high quality education to all students.

Our vision is to produce graduates who serve the nation, lead with integrity and contribute to the development of society through innovation and research.
